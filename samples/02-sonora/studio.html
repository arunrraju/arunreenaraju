[01] Principles

Three rules
on the wall

01

Measure
everything

Every unit gets 1,284 sweeps and its own sheet. If we can’t measure an improvement, we don’t ship it.

02

Build to be
opened

Torx screws, magnetic cushions, no glue. A Kontur from 2026 should still be repairable in 2046.

03

Tune by ear,
last

After the graphs agree, three tuners listen blind. Two of three must prefer the change.
