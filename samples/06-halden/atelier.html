InèsVarga

“I trained as a sculptor. I still make every piece as if it will stand alone in an empty room — because the best ones do.”

Before Forma, Inès spent nine years casting bronze in foundries outside Porto. She designs each collection in clay at full scale, sits in every maquette for a week, and only then lets the atelier draw a single line.
