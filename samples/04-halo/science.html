What each sensor reads

Small signals, big picture.

The finger’s arteries sit close to the skin, so light-based sensors read a cleaner pulse here than at the wrist.

Green PPG

Measures each heartbeat by how blood absorbs green light — the basis of heart rate and HRV.

250 HzSampling rate

Red + infrared

Compares two wavelengths to estimate blood-oxygen saturation while you sleep.

±2%SpO₂ vs pulse oximeter

Temperature

Two NTC domes track skin temperature trends across nights and cycles.
