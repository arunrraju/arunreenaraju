Chapter I — The brief

Clothes for summer the hour before the city wakes.

Concrete Psalms is our first collection built around one hour: 4am, when Berlin is just bare roads, sodium light and the hum of the ring motorway. Washed blacks, sand canvas, one reflective tape that only shows up under a flash.
