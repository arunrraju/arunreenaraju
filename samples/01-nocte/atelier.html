Julien ArceneauxMaster perfumer · 31 years at the organ
( 02 ) The nose
“A perfume is a memory you haven’t lived yet. My work is to make it feel inevitable.”

Julien trained in Versailles before spending a decade sourcing oud in Assam and Cambodia. Every Nocté extrait passes through more than 400 trial accords on his organ before one is chosen.

He still smells every raw material that enters the atelier — and rejects roughly one batch in five. “Nature is generous, but not every year.”

J. Arceneaux
