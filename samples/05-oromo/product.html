The farm story

640 families,
one hillside.

Hambela sits on the southern edge of Guji at 2,100 m, where cool nights slow the cherry down and pack it with sugar. We’ve bought from the same washing station since 2019, and pay before harvest so families aren’t forced into early sales.

“When Oromo pays early, I can hire pickers for the ripe cherry only.”
— Abebe Gutu, washing station manager
