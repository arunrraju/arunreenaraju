JUNO is named after our nonna, Giunone, who made fizzy lemon-and-mint “tonico” in a Puglian kitchen every summer. We grew up, moved to Berlin, and couldn’t find anything that tasted that alive without a sugar avalanche. So in 2021 we made our own.

How it happened

The kitchen-
table years.

From a fermenting crock that exploded on the balcony to cans in 2,400 shops. Mostly in that order.

2019

Nonna’s notebook

We find her recipe card: lemons, mint, “a little bit of patience”. The first batch tastes like a swimming pool.

2021

Batch #312

Grapefruit, hibiscus, a whisper of reishi. Pink Fizz is born and sells out at a Neukölln flea market in 3 hours.

2023

Into cans

Infinitely recyclable aluminium, art by our friend Noor, and our first three flavours on shelves in Berlin.

2026

Six flavours, 14 cities

A 28-person team, a lab in Kreuzberg, and still one rule: under 5 g sugar, always.

Botanicals

Grown, not invented.

We buy direct from 11 small farms — Sicilian citrus, Senegalese hibiscus, Indian ashwagandha — and pay 20% above fair-trade price. Every adaptogen is third-party tested, dosed to work, and named on the front of the can.
