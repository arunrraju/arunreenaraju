Designed by Marín Torres Arquitectos and finished in 2024, Halcyon is two travertine and lime-plaster volumes stacked on the last flat rock before the cliff drops to the sea. The upper volume cantilevers 8.2 metres west, so the principal suite floats over the infinity pool and sees water on two sides.

The ground floor is one 22-metre room of floor-to-ceiling glass that opens fully onto the pool deck. Below it, cut into the rock, is a spa level with a 10-metre indoor pool, hammam, gym and a cinema for eight.

Everything is built to last on the coast: marine-grade bronze fittings, solid travertine from Úbeda, 42 kW of roof solar and a 90 m³ rainwater cistern under the lawn.
