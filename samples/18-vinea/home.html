“We do very little. We wait, we listen to the weather, and we let the hill speak.”

— Hélène Vauquelin, eleventh-generation vigneronne

Since 1812, one family has tended the same slope above the Saône — fourteen hectares of limestone, old vines and patience. Every bottle is picked by hand, aged in our own oak beneath the house, and sealed in wax by the people who grew it.

The house in three colours

Rouge, rosé & bubbles.

Each colour has its own cellar and its own rhythm: eighteen months in oak for the reds, a single night on the skins for the rosé, five years on the lees for the sparkling.
