Our story · Since 1812

Eleven generations, one hill.

The Vauquelin family planted its first rows above the Saône in 1812. The house, the cellars and most of the habits have changed very little since.

1812

The first rows

Étienne Vauquelin buys a walled plot of limestone and plants Pinot Noir by hand.

1961

The Clos replanted

The vines that make our Grand Cru today go into the ground. Some are now 64 years old.

2008

Certified organic

No synthetic sprays, cover crops between rows, and horses back in the steepest parcels.

2026

The 214th harvest

Picked over eleven mornings in September. Resting in barrel now, released in 2028.

“We don’t make the wine. We keep the hill healthy, pick at the right hour, and try not to get in the way.”

Hélène Vauquelin · Vigneronne, 11th generation
