( Seven steps )

One bean, five weeks.

We follow every lot from the farm's fermentation boxes to our tempering table — and print each date inside the wrapper.

1

Harvest

DAY 0 · BY MACHETE

Only fully ripe pods, cut by hand and opened the same day.

2

Ferment

6 DAYS · 48 °C

Pulp and beans rest under banana leaves; flavour is born here.

3

Dry

10 DAYS · SUN

Turned every hour on raised beds until 7% moisture.

4

Roast

118 °C · 24 MIN

A low, slow roast in Brussels to keep the florals.

5

Winnow

SHELL OFF · NIBS

Shells crack away, leaving pure roasted nibs.

6
LIVE

Conche

72 HOURS · GRANITE

Stone wheels grind nibs and sugar to 18 microns.

7

Temper

50 → 27 → 31.5 °C

The crystal dance that gives the gloss and the snap.

( Step 7 · Tempering curve )

Why it snaps.

Melt to 50 °C to erase every crystal, cool to 27 °C on marble to seed new ones, then warm to 31.5 °C so only stable form V crystals survive. Miss by one degree and the bar turns dull and soft.
