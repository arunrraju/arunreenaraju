The company · Founder · [Year] to now

Metzee is a live-auction marketplace built for India. Buyers bid, pay and track delivery without ever leaving the stream.

How it works

Sellers broadcast live shows and run auctions in real time. Bidding, payment and delivery tracking all happen inside the stream.

Who sells

Anyone can sell: resellers, wholesalers, manufacturers and collectors alike.
